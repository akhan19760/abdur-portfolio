  1. One loud typeface, used hugeThe display face runs at 128–396px. Everything else is one quiet sans at 12–18px. That 25:1 jump in size carries most of the drama.
  2. Three colours, held toA base, one loud colour and one support colour. No gradients, no glow, no blur, even in the header.
  3. Colour comes in whole blocksA whole section turns lime, a panel turns violet, the footer is solid orange. Flat colour areas do the work that effects do on lesser sites.
  4. One shape languageEither 8px corners everywhere or pills everywhere. Every control looks like it belongs to the same family.
  5. A quiet headerA logo, three links and one filled button. No counters, no badges, no stats.
  6. One move per sectionEach section has one choreographed moment tied to scroll: a video grows, panels stack, words fill in, a line draws. The rest of the section stays still.
  7. Media, bigShowreels, photos and illustration at half a screen or more. Never a row of thumbnails.
  8. Tilt instead of effectsPanels, stickers and cards are rotated 2–8°. It adds energy with no glow at all.
  9. A voiceGame language with wit: "Choose your character", "Minimap", "Rise to the top", "Can you start yesterday?"
  10. A finale footerA full block of colour with either a giant word or a sign-off line. Nobody leaves on a link list.
  11. Off-centre gridSmall capitalised labels sit in a left column, with headlines set off to the right. Asymmetry reads as intent.
  12. Nothing left stockThe cookie banner, form, menu and 404 are all designed. Nowhere feels unfinished.
Why LootLedger reads as generated today
  • Glow on everything: neon box-shadows, drop-shadows and ember particles. Neither site uses any.
  • Blurred cover backdrops, gradient scrims and a perspective grid floor where they would use one flat colour.
  • HUD clutter: ping dots, "Live price feed", count-ups and key hints. Lots of small effects but no single idea.
  • Too many voices: three typefaces, plus four rarity colours on top of lime.
  • The box art, the only real media the site has, is never shown big.
  • Every section uses the same template: eyebrow, heading, grid of cards.
